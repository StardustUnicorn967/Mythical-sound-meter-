Calibration

By default the detector shows dBFS, a microphone signal level relative to digital full scale (0 dBFS), not environmental sound pressure. To estimate dB SPL, use a known sound-level meter as reference, adjust the offset until the readings match, and enable the checkbox. The default 100 dB offset is only a starting value, not a factory calibration. Microphone processing, device hardware and browser limitations affect accuracy.
